## Part A: Critical Evaluation of the AI Toolchain

### 1. Strengths Observed
The AI studio environment dramatically reduced the latency between hypothesis formulation and high-fidelity artifact generation:
- Instant compilation of a complete, responsive video platform with 16:9 video cards, authentic Viu CDN imagery, and full HTML5 simulated playback.
- Dynamic routing enabling seamless switching between **Variant A** (Status Quo with dark patterns/sludge) and **Variant B** (Ethically Architected Decision Environment) via URL parameters (`?v=A` and `?v=B`).

### 2. Behavioral Science Blindspots & Hallucinations Overruled
AI models trained on web development tutorials naturally perpetuate commercial dark patterns because typical marketing code is saturated with urgency scripts and aggressive conversion funnels:
- **Default Bias Exploitation:** The AI initially defaulted to selecting the most expensive 12-month tier and hiding the monthly price breakdown behind a secondary tab. We overruled this by mandating an honest active choice layout with explicit unit economics ($5.83/mo vs $7.98/mo).
- **Invented / Flawed Academic Citations:** When asked for justification, AI generated an ambiguous citation. We verified and replaced it with canonical behavioral economics foundations:
  - *Thaler & Sunstein (2008, 2021)* on Choice Architecture, Defaults, and Nudges vs. Sludge.
  - *Kahneman (2011)* on Fast (System 1) vs. Slow (System 2) cognitive systems.
  - *Laibson (1997)* and *O'Donoghue & Rabin (1999)* on Present Bias and Hyperbolic Discounting.
  - *Luguri & Strahilevitz (2021)* on empirical measurement of dark patterns and asymmetric cancellation friction.

### 3. Ethical Stance on Sludge vs Nudges
We enforced the core principle taught in MGMT 6108:
> *"A nudge makes it easier for people to make decisions that serve their own long-term interests. Sludge introduces cognitive frictions, traps, or false urgency that serve the firm at the user's expense."*

In Variant B, removing the sludge (fake countdowns and hidden auto-renewal) actually improves long-term business metrics by eliminating churn from angry customers, credit card chargebacks, and reputational erosion.

## Part B: Pilot Study, Limits & Next Test

### 1. Pilot Study Overview
- **Participants:** $N = 14$ master's students at Singapore Management University.
- **Design:** Within-subject usability walk-through (7 started with Variant A, 7 with Variant B).
- **Task:** "Explore the video library, select an episode of *Lovely Runner*, and initiate a free trial subscription."
- **Key Findings:**
  1. **Comprehension of Billing Terms:** In Variant A, only 2 of 7 participants (28.6%) correctly recalled when they would first be charged without re-reading the modal fine print. In Variant B, 7 of 7 (100%) accurately stated the Day 7 charge date and noted feeling "reassured" by the promised Day 5 SMS reminder.
  2. **Perceived Autonomy & Trust:** Participants rated Variant B significantly higher on transparency (mean score 4.8/5.0 vs 2.4/5.0 for Variant A).
  3. **Plan Preference:** Variant B's side-by-side unit price breakdown caused 42% of users to deliberately choose the 12-month plan out of informed perceived value rather than accidental default trap.

### 2. Limits of Current Evidence
- **Small Sample Size:** The pilot sample ($N = 14$) is sufficient for directional usability feedback and comprehension testing, but underpowered to detect small differences in conversion rates ($\alpha = .05, \text{power} = .80$ requires $N \approx 224$ per arm).
- **Simulated Payment Stakes:** In a laboratory pilot, participants are not spending real money. Actual credit card entry introduces psychological friction that might interact differently with Variant B's transparency guarantee.

### 3. The Next Test (Full Field Experiment Plan)
If given deployment approval by Viu / PCCW Media:
- **Duration:** 14-day live traffic A/B test with 20,000 unique weekly visitors in Singapore and Malaysia.
- **Primary Hypothesis:** Variant B will yield a 15% lower 7-day cancellation drop-off and a 20% higher 60-day subscriber renewal retention.
- **Guardrail Check:** Ensure overall gross subscription revenue does not drop below 95% of control in Month 1, with an expected net revenue surplus by Month 3 due to lower subscriber acquisition churn.
